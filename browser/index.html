racetraQ: a quantum circuit learned to drive this car

racetraQ is a quantum reinforcement-learning demo. A race car is driven by a small variational quantum circuit, trained with double deep Q-learning, next to a classical neural-network baseline. Watch the trained drivers lap the track, then race them yourself. Everything is simulated in your browser, phone included; the page needs JavaScript.
